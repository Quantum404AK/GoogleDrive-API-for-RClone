# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Quantum404AK Google Drive API** (the “Application,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use this small-scale application and its related website or documentation. The Application is an independently operated project intended for use by the owner and a limited number of known acquaintances who have chosen to use it with mutual trust and understanding.

This policy is written to be clear about the Application’s limited scope. It is not a substitute for legal advice. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Application.

## 1. Scope and role of the Application

The Application provides a limited interface for interacting with Google Drive, including use with RClone or similar personal workflows. It is **not** a general-purpose public storage platform, advertising service, data broker, or large-scale commercial product.

The Application is designed for small-scale use among known acquaintances. Access is not intended to be offered indiscriminately to the public. Even within a trusted group, users should share only information that they are comfortable processing through the Application and should follow applicable law and Google’s terms.

For Google Drive data, Google may remain the relevant service provider and controller or processor under its own policies. You should review Google’s Privacy Policy and the permissions requested by your Google account before authorizing access.

## 2. Information the Application may process

Depending on how you use the Application and how it is configured, it may process:

- **Google account and authorization information**, such as an account identifier, email address, OAuth access token, refresh token, scopes, and authorization timestamps;
- **Google Drive metadata**, such as file names, folder names, file IDs, MIME types, sizes, timestamps, permissions, and directory structure;
- **File content**, but only when a requested operation requires reading, uploading, downloading, syncing, or modifying that content;
- **Technical information**, such as IP address, browser or device information, request timestamps, error details, and basic diagnostic logs, if those are made available by the hosting environment;
- **Communications**, if you contact the operator for support, including your contact details and the contents of your message; and
- **Information you voluntarily provide**, such as a name or description used to identify an account or configuration.

The Application is not intended to collect sensitive personal information, payment information, advertising identifiers, or precise location information. Please do not submit such information unless it is necessary for your Google Drive workflow and you understand the associated risks.

## 3. How information is used

Information may be used only as reasonably necessary to:

1. authenticate and authorize Google Drive access;
2. perform the Google Drive operations you request;
3. maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the small-scale Application;
4. respond to support questions and communicate important service or policy changes;
5. detect misuse, unauthorized access, security incidents, or violations of the Terms of Service; and
6. comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests.

We do not use Google Drive content or account information for advertising, profiling, unrelated analytics, credit decisions, or data brokerage.

## 4. No sale or commercial exploitation of data

We do **not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise monetize** your personal information or Google Drive content. The Application is used only with known acquaintances under mutual trust and understanding, and it is not intended to create a market for user data.

This commitment does not prevent disclosure when you direct us to share information, when a service provider is needed to operate the Application, or when disclosure is required by law. Any service provider should receive only the access reasonably necessary for its limited function and should not use the information for its own advertising or sale.

## 5. Google API and third-party services

The Application uses Google APIs and may depend on hosting, logging, domain, or other infrastructure providers. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. The Application does not override Google Drive’s sharing settings, account security, or retention controls.

When you grant access, Google may display the requested permissions. You should grant only the minimum scopes necessary, keep your Google account secure, and revoke access from your Google Account settings when you no longer want the Application to connect.

The Application does not claim to be affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Google. “Google Drive” and related names are trademarks of Google LLC.

## 6. Storage, retention, and deletion

We aim to minimize retention. Google Drive content should generally remain in your Google Drive rather than being permanently copied into the Application. Temporary processing, caches, backups, tokens, and logs may exist when needed to complete an operation, maintain security, or diagnose an error.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, to maintain necessary records, or to satisfy legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the technical configuration of the hosting provider.

You may request deletion of information held by the operator, subject to information that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution. Deleting information from the Application does not delete files from Google Drive; use Google Drive controls for that purpose.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to a small-scale project, such as access controls, limited distribution, and protection of credentials where supported by the deployment. However, no online service, computer, network, or storage system is completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting your Google account, passwords, recovery methods, OAuth credentials, tokens, local devices, and RClone configuration. Do not publish tokens or credentials in source code, issue reports, screenshots, or chat messages. If you believe an account or token has been compromised, revoke it through Google immediately and notify the operator.

## 8. Sharing and user responsibilities

You remain responsible for the files and information in your Google Drive, the permissions you grant, and the people with whom you share files. Do not use the Application to access or distribute data without authorization, or to process unlawful, highly sensitive, or third-party confidential information without the required permission.

You should maintain your own backups. The operator is not responsible for accidental deletion, overwriting, corruption, synchronization conflicts, or loss resulting from your Google account, RClone, configuration, network, or use of the Application.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

- stop using the Application;
- revoke Google authorization through your Google Account;
- request access to or correction or deletion of personal information held by the operator;
- ask questions about how your information is handled; and
- object to or restrict processing where applicable.

To make a request, use the contact method provided by the project operator. We may need to verify your identity and may be unable to fulfill requests that would compromise another person’s privacy or conflict with a legal obligation.

## 10. Children’s privacy

The Application is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone who cannot lawfully authorize access to a Google account. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact the operator so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

The operator or infrastructure providers may process information in countries other than your own. Privacy protections in those countries may differ. By using the Application, you acknowledge that limited technical processing may occur wherever the relevant infrastructure is located, subject to applicable law.
